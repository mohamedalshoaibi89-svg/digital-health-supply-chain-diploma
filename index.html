ABOUT THE DIPLOMA

Where healthcare meets
intelligent supply chains.

The Digital Health Supply Chain Diploma is designed to strengthen the capabilities needed to plan, manage and transform healthcare supply chains in an increasingly digital environment. It brings together procurement, logistics, data, technology and healthcare operations in one applied learning journey.

PROGRAM OUTLINE

A learning journey
built around impact.

Explore the proposed learning themes. The final timetable, session sequence and durations will be confirmed by the organizer.

ⓘ Proposed agenda — subject to organizer confirmation.
01Digital health & supply chain foundations+

Digital health landscape, health supply chain architecture, stakeholders and operating models.

02Strategic procurement & sourcing+

Demand planning, procurement strategy, supplier management, contracting and value.

03Inventory, warehousing & distribution+

Inventory optimization, warehouse operations, distribution networks and product traceability.

04Digital systems, data & analytics+

Supply chain information systems, interoperability, dashboards, analytics and data governance.

05Risk management & supply chain resilience+

Risk assessment, business continuity, emergency preparedness and resilient supply strategies.

06Performance, quality & improvement+

KPIs, service levels, quality assurance, continuous improvement and applied case studies.
